## 6. Store Policies & Customer Assurance (A11)

1. **Shipping & Delivery:**
   - 2 to 4 business days for Greater Cairo and Alexandria (35 – 45 EGP).
   - 3 to 5 business days for Delta, Canal, and Upper Egypt governorates (50 – 75 EGP).
   - Free shipping automatically applied on all orders over 200 EGP (post-coupon discount).
2. **Returns & Exchanges:**
   - 14 calendar days for standard stickers and bookmarks in unworn original condition per Egyptian Consumer Protection Law.
   - Defective prints or incorrect items are replaced or fully refunded with zero return shipping fees.
   - Custom print orders are non-refundable unless there is an evident manufacturing material defect.
3. **Data Security & Privacy:**
   - Pure client-side prototype storage (`localStorage` & `IndexedDB`).
   - No tracking telemetry or unauthorized third-party scripts.
